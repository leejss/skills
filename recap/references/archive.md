# Archive mode

Create a concise, chronological record of the conversation's development. Preserve what happened and why without turning it into a verbatim transcript or adding new technical teaching.

## Preserve the record

Capture, in order:

- the initial context and intent
- the major questions or discussion phases
- proposals and alternatives that materially shaped the outcome
- points where the direction changed and the reason given
- decisions and who expressed the preference when attribution matters
- ideas that were rejected, deferred, or replaced
- the final state of agreement
- unresolved questions and uncertainty

Use short quotations only when the exact wording is important. Prefer faithful paraphrase for the rest. Do not smooth over contradictions or rewrite early uncertainty as if the final answer was obvious from the beginning.

## Do not enrich

Do not inspect project files, research external facts, add tutorials, resolve open questions, or judge whether a decision was correct merely to improve the archive. Preserve project-specific names and context that appeared in the conversation unless they expose sensitive information.

State the coverage near the top. If only a compacted summary or partial conversation is available, say that the archive reflects the accessible context and may omit exact wording or intermediate turns. Never call the record complete when completeness cannot be established.
